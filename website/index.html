How the agent works.

The side panel sends your request to the agent engine. The model uses page context to plan actions, and the browser reports the results back to the engine.

Can I choose my own model?

Yes. Open Settings to choose your provider and model. Enable page screenshots when your selected model supports images.

What does Turbo mode do?

Turbo handles routine actions automatically. Agent Smith still asks for approval on recognized submissions and ExpertOption demo orders.

What other skills are included?

Agent Smith can analyze visible charts, backtest strategies, and replay paper trades. The optional local bridge can also generate tailored resume PDFs.
