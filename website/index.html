What Agent Smith can do.

Describe what you want in plain language and follow the work in your browser.

Understand what’s on screen.

Ask questions about a page, summarize long content, or interpret visuals with a model that supports screenshots.

Research across the web.

Find relevant sources, compare information across pages, and bring the findings back into the conversation.

Work in web apps.

Navigate interfaces, use menus and controls, enter information, and carry out tasks that span several steps.

Follow and steer the work.

See what Smith is doing, give it another instruction, or ask it to continue from the current page.

Choose how actions run.

Use Review to approve page input as Smith works, or Turbo to run the requested actions automatically.

Bring your own model.

Choose a supported AI provider and model in Settings, with optional screenshot input for models that support vision.

How the agent works.

Smith uses your request and the current page to answer directly or choose a browser action. It checks the result before deciding what to do next.

Can I choose my own model?

Yes. Open Settings to choose your provider and model. Enable page screenshots when your selected model supports images.

What does Turbo mode do?

Turbo runs requested browser actions, including submissions, without an Agent Smith approval prompt. Choose Review when you want to approve actions before they run.

What other skills are included?

Agent Smith can analyze visible charts, backtest strategies, and replay paper trades. The optional local bridge can also generate tailored resume PDFs.
